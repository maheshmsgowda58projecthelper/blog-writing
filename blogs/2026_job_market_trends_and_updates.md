## Job Market Overview
The current job market situation is marked by several notable trends. According to recent reports, nearly half of 2026 graduates had a job offer before graduation, indicating a strong demand for new talent. This is a positive sign for the class of 2026, as it suggests that employers are eager to bring on fresh graduates. Furthermore, [employers expect to bring on more new hires in 2026](https://mohr.uoregon.edu/blog/2025/12/08/2026-job-market-trends-every-student-should-know), which could lead to an increase in job opportunities for those entering the workforce.
## Job Market Overview Diagram

The labor market has also shown signs of growth, with [Indeed's JPI standing 2.4% above pre-pandemic levels](https://www.hiringlab.org/2026/05/14/us-labor-market-snapshot-april-2026) in April 2026. Additionally, nonfarm payroll employment increased by 172K, as reported by the [U.S. Congress Joint Economic Committee](https://www.jec.senate.gov/public/index.cfm/republicans/employment-update). The [Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov) also noted that employment in professional and business services continued to trend up, with an unemployment rate of 4.2% in June 2026.
## Labor Market Insights
The labor market has shown signs of growth, with Indeed's Job Posting Index standing 2.4% above pre-pandemic levels, as reported by the [Indeed Hiring Lab](https://www.hiringlab.org/2026/05/14/us-labor-market-snapshot-april-2026). This increase in job postings is a positive indicator for the job market. Additionally, nonfarm payroll employment increased by 172K, according to the [U.S. Congress Joint Economic Committee](https://www.jec.senate.gov/public/index.cfm/republicans/employment-update). This growth in employment suggests a strong labor market.
## Labor Market Insights Diagram

However, there are also signs of a slowdown in the job market, with US hiring slowing sharply in June, as reported by [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-02/us-hiring-slows-sharply-curbing-recent-momentum-in-job-growth). This slowdown is a concern, but the overall labor market remains solid, with employers adding 119,000 jobs in September, according to the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvg423n377lo). The [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/05/08/business/jobs-report-economy) also reported that US employers added 115,000 jobs in April, and unemployment was steady at 4.3 percent. These numbers suggest that while the job market may be slowing down, it is still growing and creating new opportunities for job seekers.
